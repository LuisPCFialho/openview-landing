Frequently asked

Is OpenView IPTV free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use. An optional premium unlocks multiple sources at once, recordings, reminders and backup.

Does OpenView provide channels or content?

No. OpenView is only a player. You bring your own M3U or Xtream playlist — it does not host or provide any channels or content.

Which devices are supported?

Android TV, Fire TV, and Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or newer.

How do I install it on my TV?

On Android TV or Fire TV, use the Downloader app and paste the download link, or sideload the APK after allowing installation from unknown sources.

Which playlist formats are supported?

M3U and M3U8 playlist URLs, and Xtream Codes accounts.

Is my playlist private?

Yes. Your playlist stays on your device — OpenView never uploads or shares it.
