1. What the app is (and is not)

OpenView IPTV is an IPTV player. It does not provide, host, or distribute any channels, streams, or content — you add your own Xtream/M3U lines. A premium licence unlocks features of the player software; it does not include any content, channel subscription, or streaming service.

2. What is sold

  • Lifetime licence: premium software features with no expiry date, on up to 5 devices. Paid once.
  • Annual licence: premium software features on up to 2 devices. Bought on the web, it lasts 12 months from activation and does not renew. Bought in the Google Play version, it is a yearly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play.

A time-limited free trial may also be offered; it requires no payment, ends automatically, and is not a purchase. Each licence works on up to 2 devices (annual) or 5 devices (lifetime). “Lifetime” refers to the lifetime of the app and the licensed device, not a perpetual guarantee that any third-party services will keep working.

Once payment is confirmed, a unique activation code is generated and sent to the email used for the purchase, and is also made available/activated in the app on the Premium screen. Delivery is digital and usually immediate. If the code does not arrive, check your spam folder and contact support.

To free a device you no longer use (e.g. after loss or replacement), contact support.
