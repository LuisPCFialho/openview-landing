Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

OpenView IPTV (the “app”) is a media player for IPTV sources that you provide. The app does not include, host, or supply any channels, streams, or content. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

The short version

  • We do not collect your personal data.
  • We do not track what you watch: no channel, movie, series or programme names ever leave your device.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising/analytics SDKs.
  • The app sends anonymous daily usage totals to our own server (which sections and features are used), and you can turn this off in Settings.
  • Your playlists and credentials stay on your device.
  • On Google Play, purchases are processed by Google Play Billing.

Data the app handles

  • Your IPTV sources (M3U URL / Xtream login, etc.): stored only on your device and used only to connect to your provider. They are never sent to us.
  • Anonymous device identifier: a non-reversible hash derived from the Android device ID, used solely to bind a premium licence to your device. It is not linked to your name or any account.
  • Premium activation & payment: when you start a trial, buy, or activate premium, our activation server (a Cloudflare Worker) processes a one-time code and the anonymous device identifier. On the Google Play version, purchases are processed by Google Play Billing under Google's terms; on other versions payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record) — we never receive or store your card details.
  • Movie/series info (TMDB): to show posters, synopses and cast, the app sends the title of the movie/series you open to The Movie Database (TMDB). This can be turned off in Settings. See the TMDB privacy policy.
  • Updates (sideload version only): the GitHub-distributed build checks GitHub for the latest version. Store versions update through the store.
  • Usage statistics (all versions, anonymous, on by default): once a day the app tells our own server (a Cloudflare Worker and database, not a third-party analytics service) that it is being used. It sends only the one-way device code used for licences, the distribution channel (Google Play, website), the app version, whether the app is free, on trial or Premium, and daily totals of how many times each part of the app was used, for example "opened the TV Guide 3 times", "played a live channel 5 times" or "tapped a Premium feature without Premium". It never sends channel, movie, series or programme names, playlist addresses, searches, or anything you type. No account, no name, no email, no IP stored. We use these totals only to know how many people use the app and which features matter, so we can improve it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Advanced (“Usage statistics”); when it is off the app sends nothing and deletes the totals it had not sent yet.
  • Crash reports (anonymous, on by default): the app sends anonymous diagnostics after a crash — app version, device model, Android version and a stack trace with URLs and credentials removed — to help us fix bugs. No personal data is included. This is enabled by default; you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

What we never do

We do not sell or share data, profile users, record what you watch, or include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The app is designed to work without any account. This website counts daily unique visitors using a random number stored in your browser, on our own server. It also uses Matomo, hosted on our own server (not a third-party service), to see which pages are visited and where visitors come from - no cookies, and IP addresses are anonymised before they are stored.

Permissions

The app requests only what it needs to function — internet access (to reach your provider and services above), notifications (reminders), and, on the sideload build, permission to install its own update. It does not access your contacts, location, photos, or microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Parental controls (PIN) are available in Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.
